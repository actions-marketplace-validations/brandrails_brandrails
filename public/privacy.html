Brandrails privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the hosted Brandrails MCP server at brandrails.holisy.pro/mcp, which the Brandrails app in ChatGPT and other AI assistants call, and this website. The open-source CLI, GitHub Action and local MCP server run on your own machine and send nothing to us.

Who is responsible

Matej Holek, sole trader (Holisy), Bratislava, Slovakia. Contact: matej@holisy.pro.

What the MCP server receives

DataWhyHow long
Content you or your assistant send to a tool: stylesheets, a design tokens file, code or a git diff, and their file pathsTo extract tokens, generate rules files or check for hard-coded values, and return the resultOnly while the request runs. Files are written to a temporary folder that is deleted when the request ends. Nothing is stored afterwards.
Technical request data: IP address, time, URL path, response statusTo run and protect the serviceKept by our hosting provider in its platform logs for a short period under its own retention settings. We do not copy or analyse them.

The MCP server does not ask for your name, email or account, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising. Please do not send secrets or personal data in the files you check; the tools do not need them.

Website analytics and cookies

The web pages (not the MCP server) use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read, but only if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until then no analytics script loads and no cookie is set.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with Cookie settings; declining removes the analytics cookies.

Who receives it

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the server and processes the request on our behalf.
  • Google Ireland Ltd receives website analytics data, only after you consent.
  • The AI assistant you use (for example OpenAI for ChatGPT) sends your request to us and receives the result. Its handling of your conversation is covered by its own privacy policy.

We do not sell or share the data with anyone else.

Legal basis

Processing is necessary to provide the service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), for technical logs our legitimate interest in running a secure service (Article 6(1)(f)), and for website analytics your consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

Your rights and controls

Because we keep no content and no accounts, there is usually nothing to access or delete. Analytics data is not linked to your name. You can stop using the service at any time by disconnecting the app in your assistant. You can contact us about access, deletion, objection or any other GDPR right at the address above, and you can complain to the Slovak data protection authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR).

Children

The service is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes. The source of this page is public at github.com/brandrails/brandrails.
